# Welcome to the Rbanism community

Here you can find a curated set of resources, repositories and events about R for urbanists, created and maintained by the Department of Urbanism at TU Delft.

### Who we are
Rbanism is an academic community of practice that aims to empower urbanism students, researchers, and practitioners to use open-source software and open science practices to answer urban questions effectively and with confidence. Born from a passionate group fo R users from the Department of Urbanism at TU Delft in the Netherlands, Rbanism aspires to expand on the international stage.

### What we do
💡 **Training**: See our [Geospatial Data Carpentry for Urbanism](https://datacarpentry.github.io/r-intro-geospatial/) workshop.  
📦 **Software**: See our packages [rcrisp](https://github.com/CityRiverSpaces/rcrisp), [rcoins](https://github.com/CityRiverSpaces/rcoins), and [visor](https://github.com/CityRiverSpaces/visor). More to come...  
🗺️ **Challenges**: See our [#30DayMapChallenge2026](https://github.com/Rbanism/30DayMapChallenge2026), [#30DayMapChallenge2025](https://github.com/Rbanism/30DayMapChallenge2025) and [#30DayMapChallenge2024](https://github.com/Rbanism/30DayMapChallenge2024).
📝 **Publications**: See our [Guidelines and open-source toolbox for systematic literature reviews in the field of urbanism](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/w5b8v).
